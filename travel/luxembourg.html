Luxembourg Fun Facts

A tiny, affectionate page for a tiny country that somehow contains many languages, many borders, and a very large amount of personal history for me.

Many Languages

Luxembourgish, French, and German are all official languages, and daily life often moves between several languages in the same conversation.

Small Country, Big Crossroads

Luxembourg sits between France, Belgium, and Germany, which makes it feel both very local and very international at the same time.

Free Public Transport

Since 2020, public transport has been free across the country, a policy choice I always found interesting from both a practical and governance perspective.

My Pre-CMU Chapter

Before moving to Carnegie Mellon, I lived in Luxembourg and worked at EY on Business Transformations work, mostly around institutions, services, and how organizations adapt.
