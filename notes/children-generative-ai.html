Children, Safety, and Generative AI

A starting point for thinking about child well-being, platform design, and the safeguards that emerging AI systems require.

This note is a placeholder for a future thought piece on children and generative AI. A key concern is that children may encounter AI systems in contexts that feel conversational, authoritative, or emotionally responsive, even when those systems are not designed around child development or safety.

Meaningful protection requires more than content moderation after the fact. It depends on product design, age-appropriate safeguards, transparency for parents and educators, and rules that reflect how young people actually use digital tools.

The full note will develop these themes with attention to online safety, platform governance, and comparative regulatory approaches.
