Technology Policy, AI Governance, and Emerging Technologies

About

I am an incoming PhD student in Engineering and Public Policy at Carnegie Mellon University. My research interests focus on AI governance, technology policy, and the societal and regulatory implications of emerging technologies.

I previously completed an MPhil in Technology Policy at the University of Cambridge and studied International Management at King's College London, including a year abroad at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. I also worked for three years as a Business Transformation Consultant at Ernst and Young.

Research Interests

My interests include AI governance, data governance, digital regulation, online scraping, platform accountability, responsible innovation, and the political economy of emerging technologies.
